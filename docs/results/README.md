# Evaluation records

These historical records preserve the measurements, corrections and caveats used for the implementation milestones: [17 — pilot](17-pilot-results.md) evaluates similar-code search versus clustered recommendations; [19 — v1.0](19-v1-release-gates.md) records Java/Apex gates, the displayed-rank correction and its resolution; [24 — metadata verdict](24-metadata-verdict.md) compares structural and lexical search; [26 — structural metadata](26-sfmeta-structural.md) validates the tasks and records the default-mode decision; [27 — v1.1](27-v1.1-release-gate.md) records LWC gates, the shipped JS R@1 shortfall and the later component-clone improvement; [28 — v1.3 recall](28-v1.3-recall.md) records Aura/Visualforce recall and corpus-floor reruns; and [29 — final v1.2/v1.3 gates](29-v1.2-v1.3-release-gates.md) records final gates and all-language performance. Internal research notes, pilot sample dumps and local raw artifacts are not published; remaining references to them are provenance, not public links. Self-retrieval recall measures findability rather than usefulness, and these single-machine results are not performance guarantees.
