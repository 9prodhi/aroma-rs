## Why R@1 is short (note 23, internal research note, not published)

- 194 of 288 default-seed method misses are tied maxima between near-duplicate methods
  (copied handlers, lifecycle and error paths). Choosing among them needs the suffix
  that the query does not contain; a tie-break toward the benchmark source would be
  seed fitting (note 22, an internal research note, not published, already shows Java at zero margin with such a rule).
- Start slices of decorated units can begin inside a multi-line `@wire(...)` argument
  or omit the decorator (D41 stores it as leading evidence). Changing that sampling
  would also move the v1.0 Java baseline; left as a protocol limitation.
- Remaining misses: snippet repair cannot close a cut ternary, template literal or
  comma-separated declaration.

## Idiom suite validity (D48, note 25, internal research note, not published)

Task validity is checked post-holdout by `idiom --check` before any measurement. Three
unanswerable tasks were repaired without running the search (lwc01 replaced; lwc04 and
lwc18 moved within their repository). Other tasks changed only their declared witnesses,
and no query contains a required fragment. The suite was run once on the repaired tasks
(13/20); the jsmember merge left it at 13/20.
